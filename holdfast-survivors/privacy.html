Holdfast Survivors – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Holdfast Survivors ("the game") by Jeprox Games ("we") collects and why. The short version: we do not collect or store any personal information ourselves. The game shows optional ads from Google AdMob and sells optional in-app purchases through Google Play; those Google services handle some data as described below.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (coins, upgrades, heroes, maps, best times, daily reward streak, settings, and which in-app purchases you own) in a file on your device only. We have no server and never receive this file. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it; purchases can be restored from Google Play.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you choose to watch one (for example to revive or to double your coins). The game does not show ads you did not ask for. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID and basic device information (model, operating system, language),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • information about the ads shown and whether you interacted with them.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice any time with Privacy options in the game's base screen. Elsewhere you can limit personalized ads or reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

If you buy Remove Ads, no ads are shown.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

Purchases are made and paid through Google Play. We never see your payment details. Google Play tells the game which products your account owns so it can unlock them and restore them after a reinstall. Google's handling of your payment information is described in Google's Privacy Policy and the Google Payments privacy notice.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • It contains no analytics or tracking software of our own.
  • We do not share or sell any information to anyone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and to talk to Google Play for purchases.
  • Advertising ID and Android ad services (attribution, topics): used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: added by Google's ad library for its own background work.
  • Google Play billing: to make in-app purchases.
  • Vibration: to buzz your phone when you are hit or a boss appears. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Google gives us anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences with parental guidance (Google's "PG" rating). We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example an online leaderboard), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
