Holdfast Survivors – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Holdfast Survivors ("the game") by Jeprox Games ("we") collects. The short version: the game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information.

What the game stores

The game saves your progress (coins, upgrades, heroes, maps, best times, daily reward streak, and settings) in a file on your device only. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • It does not connect to the internet and has no internet permission.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software.
  • It does not share or sell any information to anyone.

Permissions

The only permission the game uses is vibration, to buzz your phone when you are hit or a boss appears. You can turn it off in the game's settings.

Google Play

When you download the game from Google Play, Google may collect information as described in Google's Privacy Policy. Google gives us anonymous, combined statistics such as install counts and crash reports. These do not identify you.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Because the game collects no personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example optional ads, in-app purchases, or an online leaderboard), we will update this policy before that version is released and change the date above.
